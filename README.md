> **IEEE PES Tunisia General Meeting (PES TGM 7.0)** — National Solar Challenge · 5-member MedTech SMU team · 2026

Tunisia's grid operator runs about **630 MW of rooftop solar it cannot see** — small installations on
homes and businesses, unmetered at the level that matters. DustCast forecasts that production
**district by district**, with the uncertainty attached.

## 🎯 Why it matters

The costly moment isn't midday — it's **sunset**, when demand climbs while solar drops in the same
three hours. Demand is already forecast; rooftop solar is not. DustCast closes that gap.

## ✨ What it does

| Capability | Scope |
|---|---|
| Forecasts at three scales | **266 districts → 24 governorates → nation** |
| Covers homes and businesses | Low and medium voltage, three segments |
| Horizons | Same day up to three days ahead, error published per horizon |
| Uncertainty | Gives a range, not a single number |
| Self-improving | Five models, retrained monthly, tested on a 19-month replay |
| Integration | Documented HTTP interface + dashboard + report from one forecast |
